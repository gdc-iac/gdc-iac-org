Slide 1 of 7 • Executive Problem Statement

Why Traditional Cloud Sizing Fails in Disconnected SCIF Enclaves

Public cloud assumes infinite elasticity and 30-second API provisioning. Google Distributed Cloud Air-Gapped (GDC-ag) operates under strict physical, security, and supply-chain constraints.

FAILURE MODE 1 🗣️

Customer Intimidation & Guesswork BOMs

End customers fall into two distinct groups: Track A (technical teams with exact VM/pod BOMs) and Track B (mission owners who know their operational problem but freeze when asked for Kubernetes vCPUs, pgvector RAM, or FP16 KV-cache VRAM).

Result: Arbitrary hardware guesses that either waste millions or crash on Day 1.
FAILURE MODE 2 💾

The Hidden 22.2x Storage & Overhead Trap

Naive sizing only counts raw database files. In GDC-ag, 1 GiB of baseline data requires 22.2 GiB of physical Ceph disk when accounting for HNSW indexes (+20%), active DR site mirroring (×2), 14-day local snapshots (×3.7), and 3-way Ceph replication (×3).

Result: Racks run out of physical disk 90 days into production.
FAILURE MODE 3 ⏳

The 9–18 Month Procurement Blind Spot

Infrastructure Operators (IOs) allocate Day 0 (T0) capacity from existing racks without visibility into Month 12 (T+12m) growth. Because physical rack delivery, TEMPEST inspection, and SCIF accreditation take 9 to 18 months, waiting until racks fill up guarantees an outage.

Result: IO teams must trigger Month 12 Purchase Orders ON DAY 0.
The GDC-ag Unified Sizing & Procurement Solution
A Two-Layer SCIF Intake Process + Deterministic Calculation Engine that translates plain-English mission interviews into exact 4-Horizon IO Purchase Orders and Hardware-Isolated NVIDIA MIG topologies.

Anatomy of the 22.2x Raw Physical Ceph Storage Multiplier

Why 1,908 GiB (1.86 TiB) of baseline application & container data requires 54.60 TiB of physical Ceph disk on Day 0.

1.86 TiB
1,908 GiB Raw Data

STEP 2 • INDEXES (×1.2)
2.24 TiB
+20% Index Overhead

HNSW vector embeddings (`pgvector`), PostgreSQL WAL logs, and B-Tree index structures.

STEP 3 • DR + CONF (×2.2)
4.92 TiB
Usable Capacity

Active DR Site Mirror (`×2.0`) + `DERIVED` Data Confidence Contingency Buffer (`×1.10`).

STEP 4 • SNAPSHOTS (×3.7)
18.20 TiB
With Local Backups

14 days of snapshots at 5%/day change (`0.7x`) + 2 base full backups (`2.0x`) = `+2.7x` backup pool.

STEP 5 • CEPH (×3.0)
54.60 TiB
IO Raw Ceph OSD Disk

3-way physical Ceph replication across failure domains. (29.3x raw-to-baseline ratio).

Raw Ceph TiB = [ (Baseline + Harbor + DailyIngest × Days) × 1.20 (Index) × 2.0 (DR) × 1.10 (Confidence) ] × 3.70 (Snapshots) × 3.0 (Ceph 3x)
